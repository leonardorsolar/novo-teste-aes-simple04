Write a short PRD for this job: the product view, not the technical one.

Sections, in this order:

- `## Summary`: 3 to 5 bullets a human can skim.
- `## Problem`: who has it, how they work around it today, why it matters now.
- `## Users and scenarios`: the main user types and 2 to 4 concrete scenarios.
- `## Requirements`: numbered, each one observable by a user. Mark must-have vs nice-to-have.
- `## Out of scope`: what this job will not do, including what intake left out.
- `## Success`: how we will know it worked (a metric, a behaviour, or a check a person can do).
- `## Open questions`: decisions only a person can make. If one blocks the work, write
  `VERDICT: BLOCKED` on the first line instead and explain it here.

Keep it to one page. Intake's Scope is binding.
